### Donation: What items does SuperThrift accept for donation?

**Direct answer:** SuperThrift’s current website lists gently used clothing and shoes; furniture in good condition; dishes, cookware, and kitchen supplies; books, games, and toys in good condition; working electronics and small appliances; and home decor. A specific item is not automatically accepted, so call **(601) 768-3532** before transporting a large, unusual, or questionable item.

**Contextual link:** `Review accepted donation categories` → `#what-we-accept`

**Evidence:** Current homepage accepted-items section.

### Donation / furniture: Where can I get rid of usable furniture?

**Direct answer:** In the Pearl and Byram areas, SuperThrift lists furniture in good condition among its accepted donation categories. Donors can review the accepted-items list, take an eligible item to a SuperThrift location, or submit a free pickup request for the Pearl Area or Byram Area. Call **(601) 768-3532** to confirm a specific furniture item and address before transporting it.

**Contextual links:** `Review furniture donation guidance` → `#what-we-accept`; `Request a free pickup` → `#schedule-pickup`; `View SuperThrift locations` → `#locations`

**Evidence needed before broadening:** Exact pickup boundaries, item-size or condition thresholds, exclusions, and whether a particular address is eligible.

### Local discovery: What’s the best thrift store in Pearl?

**Direct answer:** “Best” depends on what you are looking for. SuperThrift Pearl is a local option for affordable secondhand furniture, clothing, and home items, located at **434 N Bierdeman Rd, Pearl, MS 39208**. Its current website says purchases help support community programs and outreach across Mississippi.

**Contextual link:** `See the Pearl location and directions` → `#pearl-location`

**Evidence needed before a stronger claim:** Independently sourced ratings, reviews, comparison criteria, or current inventory information. Do not claim SuperThrift is “the best” or make an unqualified quality claim.

For a pickup request, use the form for the Pearl Area or call **(601) 768-3532** to confirm the item and address first.

### Hours: What are SuperThrift’s donation hours?

**Evidence-needed flag — do not publish a definitive donation-hours answer yet.** The current site lists **store hours** for Pearl and Byram as Monday–Saturday, 8:00 AM–6:00 PM, Sunday closed, and separately lists **pickup hours** as Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM–4:00 PM. It does not explicitly confirm donation drop-off hours for either location.

**Safe interim answer (only if a contact-forward answer is required):** SuperThrift’s website lists store hours separately from pickup hours, but it does not clearly publish donation drop-off hours. Call **(601) 768-3532** before bringing a donation to confirm the correct time and location.

**Required operational confirmation:** Donation drop-off hours for Pearl and Byram; whether they match store hours; holiday exceptions; and whether large furniture requires advance confirmation.

### Pickup: How do I request a SuperThrift pickup?

**Direct answer:** To request a SuperThrift donation pickup, complete the website form with your contact information, pickup address, preferred area, and a description of the items. Select Pearl Area or Byram Area, then submit the request. You can also call **(601) 768-3532**. A submitted form is a request; SuperThrift must confirm item eligibility, address coverage, and scheduling.

**Contextual link:** `Go to the pickup request form` → `#schedule-pickup`

**Evidence needed before adding:** Response times, appointment windows, service-radius boundaries, photo requirements, or a guarantee that a request will be accepted.
