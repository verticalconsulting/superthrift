Donation and Furniture Pickup FAQ

Quick answers for donors in Pearl, Byram, and nearby communities. When an item or address is uncertain, call (601) 768-3532 before transporting it.

What items does SuperThrift accept for donation?

SuperThrift’s current website lists gently used clothing and shoes; furniture in good condition; dishes, cookware, and kitchen supplies; books, games, and toys in good condition; working electronics and small appliances; and home decor. Call (601) 768-3532 to confirm a specific item before transporting it.

See all listed donation categories

Does SuperThrift offer free furniture pickup?

The current website offers a free donation-pickup request for the Pearl Area and Byram Area. Pickup is requested through the website form or by calling (601) 768-3532. The site does not guarantee that every item, address, or requested time can be accommodated.

Request a free pickup

What areas does SuperThrift serve for pickup?

The pickup form currently offers Pearl Area and Byram Area as selections. Exact geographic boundaries are not published, so donors outside or near the edge of those areas should call (601) 768-3532 before submitting a request.

How do I request a donation pickup?

Complete the website form with your contact information, pickup address, preferred area, and a description of the items. You can also call (601) 768-3532. Submitting the form requests a pickup; scheduling and item eligibility should be confirmed directly with SuperThrift.

Go to the pickup form

Where can I donate old furniture in Byram?

SuperThrift Byram is listed at 6787 S Siwell Rd STE D, Byram, MS 39272. Current posted hours are Monday–Saturday, 8:00 AM–6:00 PM, with Sunday closed. Furniture should be in good condition, and donors should call (601) 768-3532 to confirm a specific piece before transporting it.
