What Personal Data do we collect from you and why?

Through your visitation to, use of, and interaction with our website, you will be asked for certain types of Personal Data. This section will only cover Personal Data that we receive specifically from you.

1. Personal Data collected through contact forms:

When you submit a donation pickup request or contact form, we collect: name, phone number, email address, pickup address, preferred location, and donation item details.

Why do we collect this Personal Data? We collect this information for the sole and exclusive purpose of scheduling donation pickups and providing our thrift store services to you.

2. Personal Data collected through your use of our website:

When you use our website, we may collect information such as pages visited, time spent on pages, and other usage statistics.

Why do we collect this Personal Data? We collect this data to improve our website experience and better serve our community.

3. Personal Data from cookies:

We use cookies. Cookies are small files stored on your computer or mobile device which collect information about your browsing behavior. Automatic Data may include information such as your computer's Internet Protocol ("IP") address, browser type, browser version, the pages of our service that you visit, the time and date of your visit, and other statistics.

Why do we collect this Personal Data? We use cookies to help us remember information about your visit, save your preferences for future visits, and to analyze traffic and usage patterns so that we can continue to improve our services.

What can I do about cookies? Most Internet browsers accept cookies automatically, although you are able to change your browser settings to control cookies, including whether or not you accept them, and to remove them. Visit http://www.whatarecookies.com for further information. We honor Do Not Track signals and do not track, plant cookies, or use advertising when a Do Not Track (DNT) browser mechanism is in place.

What is our legal basis for processing your Personal Data?

We respect data minimization principles. We only collect the minimal amount of Personal Data required for legitimate business purposes. Before you use any of our services, you'll be directed to this Privacy Policy. We'll ask you to indicate that you've read this Privacy Policy in full and agree to the processing of your voluntary Personal Data as we've described here.

Will your Personal Data ever be shared?

We use third-party service providers to help us operate our website:

  • Formspree: To handle form submissions and email delivery
  • Cloudflare Pages: To host our website

These third-party service providers will access your Personal Data only on an "if needed" basis. We may have to disclose your Personal Data in certain circumstances:

  1. To satisfy any local, state, or Federal laws or regulations
  2. To respond to requests from law enforcement or other governmental bodies
  3. To bring legal action against a user who has violated the law
  4. In the case of any business transfer, sale, or transfer of assets
  5. To generally cooperate with any lawful investigation
  6. If we suspect any fraudulent activity on our website

We do not share, sell, or otherwise provide your Personal Data to any third-party advertisers.

Do we ever send you marketing communications?

We may send you marketing communications, such as newsletters, but only after we obtain your explicit consent. You can revoke your consent at any time by:

  1. Clicking on the "unsubscribe" link contained in each marketing email sent to you; or
  2. Sending an email to marketing1@mercyhouseatc.com

How do we store and protect your Personal Data?

Personal Data Storage: We only store your Personal Data as long as it is necessary for providing you with the requested services or until you request deletion of your data.

Personal Data Protection: We employ organizational and technical security measures to protect your Personal Data, such as limiting access to your Personal Data, secured networks, encryption, and anonymization. We use secure physical and digital systems to store your Personal Data.

Please note, however, that no system involving the transmission of information via the Internet is completely secure. Although we take the protection and storage of your Personal Data very seriously, we cannot be responsible for data breaches that occur outside of our reasonable control. We will follow all applicable laws in the event a data breach occurs, including notifying you as soon as possible.

What are your rights in relation to your Personal Data?

By using our website, you can exercise the following rights:

  1. REFUSING TO PROVIDE YOUR PERSONAL DATA: You can choose to forego the provision of data, but you may be restricted from using some or all of our services.
  2. ACCESSING, OBTAINING, MODIFYING, AND DELETING YOUR PERSONAL DATA: If you wish to access, obtain, modify, or delete any Personal Data we may have about you, contact us at info@mercyhouseatc.com. We will reply within two (2) weeks.
  3. SUBMITTING A COMPLAINT: Contact us using the details in this Privacy Policy. We will investigate and respond within two (2) weeks.

What happens if we modify this Privacy Policy?

We reserve the right to modify, revise, or otherwise amend this Privacy Policy at any time. If we make any significant changes, we'll let you know and seek your consent. We'll also change the date this Privacy Policy was last modified at the top of this document.

Mississippi Privacy Rights

As a Mississippi-based organization serving Mississippi residents, we are committed to protecting your privacy rights under applicable state and federal laws. Mississippi residents have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we collect about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt-out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell or share personal information)
  • Receive information about our data collection and processing practices

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at marketing1@mercyhouseatc.com or call us at (601) 768-3532. We will respond to your request within a reasonable timeframe, but no later than 30 days.

California Privacy Rights

California Civil Code Section § 1798.83 permits users of our website that are California residents to request certain information regarding our disclosure of personal information to third parties for their direct marketing purposes. To make such a request, please send an email to marketing1@mercyhouseatc.com.

Do we collect any Personal Data from minors?

We do not allow the use of our website or any of our services by users under the age of 18. As such, we don't collect, store, or otherwise use any Personal Data from any minors. If you are a parent or guardian and learn that your children have provided us with Personal Data, please contact us at info@mercyhouseatc.com.
