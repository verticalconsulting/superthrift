What items does SuperThrift accept for donation?

Call or submit the form to confirm whether a specific item can be accepted, and to request pickup. SuperThrift accepts a range of household items that are clean, complete, and in good, sellable condition.

👕

Clothing & Shoes

Gently used clothing and shoes in clean, wearable condition.

🪔

Furniture

Furniture in good condition with all parts present.

🍳

Kitchenware

Dishes, glassware, utensils, and small kitchen items.

📚

Books & Toys

Books and toys in good condition, complete with all pieces.

🔌

Small Appliances

Working small appliances and small electronics.

🏨

Home Decor

Lamps, wall art, and decorative household items.

Request a Donation Pickup

Request a free donation pickup: Complete the form for a Pearl Area or Byram Area address. Submitting the form is a request; SuperThrift will need to confirm the item, location, and scheduling details.

  1. Enter your contact information and pickup address.
  2. Select Pearl Area or Byram Area.
  3. Describe the items you want to donate.
  4. Submit the request, or call to confirm a specific item or address.
